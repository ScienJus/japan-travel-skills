# Toyama Prefecture, Takaoka and Toyama City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checks: 2026-11-02 (JST). Source checks are not publisher update times or site-wide
validation. Keep 富山県 / Toyama Prefecture, 富山市 / Toyama City and 高岡市 / Takaoka City distinct.
Takaoka and Toyama City share the prefectural source; they have separate municipal portals.

## Shared prefectural discovery

[とやま観光ナビ](https://www.info-toyama.com/) is the official Toyama prefectural tourism portal.
Its footer identifies 富山県観光推進局 and 公益社団法人 とやま観光推進機構. Use it for wider discovery
and cross-city context, with an explicit destination/area filter or address check.

| Need | Entry and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Municipal scope | [県内観光エリアガイド](https://www.info-toyama.com/stories/15city-top): select the actual municipality |
| Takaoka within the prefecture | [高岡市エリア観光ガイド](https://www.info-toyama.com/stories/15city-takaoka): a city-specific discovery page with wider transport context |
| Attractions | [スポット・体験](https://www.info-toyama.com/attractions): confirm municipality on each chosen detail |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.info-toyama.com/events): verify venue and year/date; a prefectural listing is not a Toyama City listing |
| Routes, access and themes | Follow モデルコース, アクセス and 特集 from the portal; stated travel estimates need dated operator confirmation |

These entry bodies were readable in the source check. The portal links foreign-language versions
on `visit-toyama-japan.com`; follow the current portal link rather than guessing translated paths.
Seasonal articles, personal writer reports and official facility records serve different purposes.

## Takaoka City

Primary portal: [高岡観光ナビ](https://www.takaoka.or.jp/). The footer identifies 公益社団法人
高岡市観光協会 and 高岡市観光交流課. Use it for Takaoka sightseeing and native local links.

| Need | Entry and verification limit |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions/experiences | [観光スポット・体験](https://www.takaoka.or.jp/viewpoint/index.html): official navigation link; detail/listing body not established in the source check |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.takaoka.or.jp/event/index.html): entry body readable; verify each venue's municipality because listings can include other Toyama areas |
| Route ideas | [モデルコース](https://www.takaoka.or.jp/course/index.html): entry body readable; city branding does not bound every suggested excursion |
| Local access | [アクセス](https://www.takaoka.or.jp/access/index.html): official navigation link; body not established in the source check; transport claims need an accessible current source |
| Maps and visitor support | Follow パンフレット and 観光案内所 from the portal; check each PDF's issue date |

The homepage and the directory links are established, but some bodies are unverified. If a source
is denied or unavailable, stop that source and disclose the missing evidence. The independently
identified prefectural Takaoka page can provide its own attributed discovery information; do not
say it confirms a municipal page that could not be read.

For shortlisted temples, museums, gardens or workshops, follow the detail's actual operator link
for admission, closures and appointment conditions. A city/prefectural description is not a
confirmation of available workshop or admission slots. Do not submit booking or contact forms.

## Toyama City

Primary portal: [富山市公式観光サイト](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/), operated by
富山市観光協会. The [municipal government's tourism page](https://www.city.toyama.lg.jp/bunka/kanko/index.html)
explicitly links it. This is separate from the prefectural `info-toyama.com` portal.

| Need | Entry and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| City districts | [エリアから探す](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/search-area/): distinguish city-center and other municipal areas |
| Attractions | [観光スポット](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/spot/): read selected facility records and their operator links |
| Access | [交通アクセス](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/access/): orientation; operator service/date checks remain separate |
| Maps and visitor guides | [パンフレット](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/pamphlet/): inspect each document's edition/date, mode and geography |
| Nearby destinations | [富山の近隣観光紹介](https://www.toyamashi-kankoukyoukai.jp/around/): expressly wider than Toyama City; label those destinations as excursions |
| Events and route ideas | Follow the current homepage's event/model-route navigation; discover the native entry instead of inventing a URL |

These city entry bodies were readable in the source check. The city's municipal area includes
districts beyond the station/center; an area name alone is not a travel-time promise. A tourism
portal's view of 立山連峰 or nearby trip does not establish that the described place lies in Toyama
City or that a seasonal mountain route is operating. Verify location and responsible operators
before using such material in the itinerary.
