# Fukuoka Prefecture and Fukuoka City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


Distinguish 福岡市 / Fukuoka City, 博多 / Hakata within the city, and 福岡県 / Fukuoka Prefecture.
Dazaifu, Itoshima and Kitakyushu are wider excursions, even when a city portal lists them.

## Prefectural discovery

[クロスロードふくおか](https://www.crossroadfukuoka.jp/) is the official prefectural visitor
portal. The [Fukuoka prefectural government's visitor-information page](https://www.pref.fukuoka.lg.jp/contents/kankoubussan.html)
links it. Use its area, event and food entries when the requested trip extends beyond the city.

## Fukuoka City

Primary portal: [よかなび](https://yokanavi.com/). Its [About page](https://yokanavi.com/about)
identifies the Fukuoka city visitor-information role and 公益財団法人福岡観光コンベンションビューロー
as the secretariat.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions and experiences | [観光スポット](https://yokanavi.com/spots): check area and address; nearby cities also appear |
| Events | [イベント](https://yokanavi.com/events): preserve year, venue and applicable notices |
| Food culture | [福岡・博多グルメ](https://yokanavi.com/gourmet/): introductions and local specialties |
| Yatai | Follow 屋台 from the primary portal; obtain venue rules and actual operating information before assigning a visit |
| Routes | [モデルコース](https://yokanavi.com/routes): selected city and nearby-area sequences |
| City transport | [市内交通ガイド](https://yokanavi.com/trafficguide): practical orientation; use dated operator evidence for connections |

Portal rankings, user travel logs and AI-generated course features are different from facility or
operator notices. Prefer the latter for actual opening and access. A yatai introduction does not
guarantee it operates on the intended night. Read-only research does not use the portal's booking
or saved-account workflows.
