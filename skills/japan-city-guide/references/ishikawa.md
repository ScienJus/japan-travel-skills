# Ishikawa Prefecture and Kanazawa

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checks: 2026-11-02 (JST). This identifies the source/link check, not publication
freshness or site-wide validation. Japanese pages are the starting references; follow current
language links from the official portal when needed and retain the source version used.

Use the Kanazawa entry for city sightseeing. Use the prefectural portal for a deliberately wider
Ishikawa search. A day trip can cross prefectural boundaries; verify actual destination scope.

## Prefectural discovery

- [ほっと石川旅ねっと](https://www.hot-ishikawa.jp/): Ishikawa's official tourism portal, operated
  by 石川県観光連盟. Useful for prefectural area discovery, attractions, seasonal features and events.
- Official identity evidence: the [Ishikawa prefectural government's tourism page](https://www.pref.ishikawa.lg.jp/kanko_koryu/kanko/index.html)
  identifies and links this portal; [Kanazawa's related links](https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/links/index.html)
  also identify the prefectural tourism body.
- Verification limit: official identity is supported, but direct homepage text retrieval was not
  established in the entry check. Discover the necessary page through the official navigation or
  targeted site search. If its current body cannot be read, leave dependent facts unverified;
  snippets do not establish current event dates or opening conditions.

## Kanazawa City

Primary portal: [金沢旅物語](https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/), 金沢市観光協会's official
Kanazawa tourism site. Municipal discovery is its main role; nearby-trip suggestions are not all
inside Kanazawa or Ishikawa. Homepage and the following city directory bodies were readable in
the entry check.

| Need | Entry and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions and activities | [観光・体験](https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/spot/index.html): categories, city areas and native records; check the record's address and scope |
| Events for intended dates | [イベント](https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/event/index.html): locate the correct year's event detail, then the organizer's notices |
| Sequencing and visit ideas | [モデルコース](https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/modelCourse/index.html): inspect the route's mode, duration assumptions and geography |
| Local access and maps | Follow アクセス and 観光サポート from the portal; use operator schedules for a concrete travel date |
| Current city themes | Follow 特集 from the portal; preserve year/date and distinguish editorial guidance from current operating facts |

### Following a facility to its responsible source

The [兼六園 record](https://www.kanazawa-kankoukyoukai.or.jp/spot/detail_10106.html) is a useful
example: it separates address, ordinary/early access, visit-duration estimates and an official
facility link labelled 金沢城と兼六園. Follow that link to the prefectural garden/park manager for
current operating conditions. Its location in a prefectural management site does not make the
garden a prefecture-wide destination.

Do not copy this record's price or hours into the skill. Its viewing-season descriptions do not
predict the user's travel-date foliage. Night opening/light-up dates need their own current event
record; the ordinary daytime rule alone does not establish night admission. Other shortlisted
facilities should be verified through their own listed managers in the same way.
