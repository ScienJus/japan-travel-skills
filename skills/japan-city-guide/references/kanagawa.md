# Kanagawa Prefecture and Yokohama City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


横浜市 / Yokohama City is distinct from 神奈川県 / Kanagawa Prefecture. Kamakura, Hakone,
Fujisawa/Enoshima and Yokosuka require explicitly wider discovery and their own connections.

## Prefectural discovery

[観光かながわNOW](https://www.kanagawa-kankou.or.jp/) is the official prefectural visitor
portal. Its footer identifies 公益社団法人神奈川県観光協会. Use it for wider destination and
area discovery, not as a city-only list.

## Yokohama City

Primary portal: [横浜市観光情報サイト](https://www.welcome.city.yokohama.jp/).
Its footer and linked [association information](https://business.yokohamajapan.com/ja/)
identify 公益財団法人横浜市観光協会 / Yokohama City Visitors Bureau.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions | [観光スポット](https://www.welcome.city.yokohama.jp/spot/): select the city area and inspect the actual record |
| Events | [イベント検索](https://www.welcome.city.yokohama.jp/eventinfo/): locate the dated occurrence, then its organizer |
| Food and restaurants | [レストランガイド](https://www.welcome.city.yokohama.jp/gourmet/): discovery; sponsored/editorial selections are not independent ratings |
| Routes | [おすすめコース](https://www.welcome.city.yokohama.jp/courses/): check walking distances, transport assumptions and entry conditions |
| Access | [アクセス・交通情報](https://www.welcome.city.yokohama.jp/access/): general movement guidance |
| Maps and practical support | Follow 観光案内所 and 横浜観光バリアフリー情報 from the portal |

The port, central districts and other city areas are not one compact walking location. Confirm
the selected facility's address and admission separately. Cruise schedules, fireworks and seasonal
illuminations require current operator/organizer notices. The portal links language versions on
`yokohamajapan.com`; preserve the version read and do not assume simultaneous updates.
