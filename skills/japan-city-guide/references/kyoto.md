# Kyoto Prefecture and Kyoto City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


Use 京都市 / Kyoto City for the city itinerary. Uji, Amanohashidate, Ine and other prefectural
excursions are not Kyoto City; add them only with the requested wider scope and feasible transfers.

## Prefectural discovery

[京都府観光連盟](https://www.kyoto-kankou.or.jp/) provides prefectural discovery. Its
[organization page](https://www.kyoto-kankou.or.jp/hq/about) identifies 公益社団法人京都府観光連盟.
Use its area entries for the coast, forests, tea-producing area and other places beyond the city.

## Kyoto City

Primary portal: [Kyoto Travel — Kyoto City Official Guide](https://kyoto.travel/en/).
The footer and [About Us](https://kyoto.travel/en/about-us) identify the City of Kyoto and
京都市観光協会 / Kyoto City Tourism Association (DMO KYOTO). The English entries below were read;
use current language links rather than assuming translated versions have identical content.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Districts and facilities | [Destinations](https://kyoto.travel/en/destinations): select a district and follow the facility's own site |
| Events | [Festivals & Events](https://kyoto.travel/en/events): preserve the occurrence's year and admission conditions |
| Food culture | [Food & Drink](https://kyoto.travel/en/food-and-drink): meal and culture discovery; verify individual restaurant conditions separately |
| Routes | [Sample Itineraries](https://kyoto.travel/en/itineraries): ideas for sequencing, not date-specific connection proof |
| Local movement | [Getting Around Kyoto](https://kyoto.travel/en/getting-around): public-transport orientation |
| Luggage, maps and visitor support | Follow Luggage Services, Maps & Tools and Tourist Information Center from Kyoto Travel |

Temple special openings, night visits and ordinary daytime access can have different conditions.
Use the temple, garden or event organizer's current notice for the selected visit. Keep source
accessibility separate from whether admission is available. Responsible-travel guidance is part
of planning; access or photography restrictions cannot be overridden by a model itinerary.
