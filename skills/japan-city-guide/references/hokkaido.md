# Hokkaido and Sapporo City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


札幌市 / Sapporo City is one destination within Hokkaido. Otaru, Hakodate, Asahikawa and Furano
are separate municipalities; a Hokkaido-wide result is not a Sapporo city match.

## Prefectural discovery

[HOKKAIDO LOVE!](https://www.visit-hokkaido.jp/) is the official Hokkaido visitor portal.
Its [site information](https://www.visit-hokkaido.jp/aboutsite) and footer identify
公益社団法人北海道観光機構. Use it for deliberate cross-city and regional discovery, retaining
travel distances and the actual municipality.

## Sapporo City

Primary portal: [ようこそさっぽろ](https://www.sapporo.travel/). The footer and linked
[association information](https://association.sapporo.travel/about/) identify 札幌観光協会.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions | [観光スポット](https://www.sapporo.travel/spot/): facility directory and area discovery |
| Food themes | [グルメ特集](https://www.sapporo.travel/gourmet/feature/): local food experiences; follow individual venue conditions separately |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.sapporo.travel/event/): use the intended year and event organizer |
| Planning context | [旅のお役立ち](https://www.sapporo.travel/info/about/): city basics and links to seasonal, map and visitor-support information |
| Transport | [交通情報](https://www.sapporo.travel/info/about/transportation/): arrival and movement orientation; verify dated services with operators |
| Maps and support | Follow 札幌観光マップ and 観光案内 from the portal |

Do not classify Jozankei as outside Sapporo merely because it is away from the center. Conversely,
a regional excursion needs its own transfer plan. Winter articles give seasonal preparation,
not a forecast for the user's date. Check current event, trail, lift or transport notices where
snow or seasonal operation affects a selected activity.
