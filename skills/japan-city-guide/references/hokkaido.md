# Hokkaido destination sources

## Scope and source checks

Regional and Sapporo portal entries checked on 2026-11-05 (JST). Facility and other destination entries checked on
2026-11-25 (JST). Reopen the selected record for date-specific access and operating notices.

札幌市 / Sapporo City is one destination within Hokkaido. Otaru, Hakodate, Asahikawa and Furano
are separate municipalities; a Hokkaido-wide result is not a Sapporo city match.

## Prefectural discovery

[HOKKAIDO LOVE!](https://www.visit-hokkaido.jp/) is the official Hokkaido visitor portal.
Its [site information](https://www.visit-hokkaido.jp/aboutsite) and footer identify
公益社団法人北海道観光機構. Use it for deliberate cross-city and regional discovery, retaining
travel distances and the actual municipality.

## Sapporo City

Primary portal: [ようこそさっぽろ](https://www.sapporo.travel/). The footer and linked
[association information](https://association.sapporo.travel/about/) identify 札幌観光協会.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions | [観光スポット](https://www.sapporo.travel/spot/): facility directory and area discovery |
| Food themes | [グルメ特集](https://www.sapporo.travel/gourmet/feature/): local food experiences; follow individual venue conditions separately |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.sapporo.travel/event/): use the intended year and event organizer |
| Planning context | [旅のお役立ち](https://www.sapporo.travel/info/about/): city basics and links to seasonal, map and visitor-support information |
| Transport | [交通情報](https://www.sapporo.travel/info/about/transportation/): arrival and movement orientation; verify dated services with operators |
| Maps and support | Follow 札幌観光マップ and 観光案内 from the portal |
| [札幌もいわ山 / Mount Moiwa](https://mt-moiwa.jp/guide/) | Ropeway and cable car hours, fares and service status. |

Do not classify Jozankei as outside Sapporo merely because it is away from the center. Conversely,
a regional excursion needs its own transfer plan. Winter articles give seasonal preparation,
not a forecast for the user's date. Check current event, trail, lift or transport notices where
snow or seasonal operation affects a selected activity.

## Other destinations

Look up the municipality or visitor area by name. For an attraction with its own operator, use
the destination portal for context and the operator for current admission or service notices.

| Destination | Official sources and available information |
| --- | --- |
| 旭川市 / Asahikawa | [旭川観光コンベンション協会](https://www.atca.jp/) for local visitor guides. [旭川市旭山動物園](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/asahiyamazoo/) for the zoo calendar and animal-viewing notices. |
| 美瑛町 / Biei | [美瑛町観光協会](https://www.biei-hokkaido.jp/ja/) for local sights and access, including [白金青い池](https://www.biei-hokkaido.jp/ja/shirogane-blue-pond) seasonal access and [クリスマスツリーの木](https://www.biei-hokkaido.jp/ja/facility/christmas-tree) viewing rules and road restrictions. |
| 千歳市 / Chitose | [千歳市 visitor guide](https://www.welcome-to-chitose.jp/guide.html) covers visitor desks at Chitose Station and Lake Shikotsu. |
| 富良野市 / Furano | [ふらの観光協会 access guide](https://www.furanotourism.com/jp/static/access.php) covers local and regional transport from Furano Station, including seasonal services. |
| 函館市 / Hakodate | [はこぶら](https://www.hakobura.jp/) for city sights, food and neighborhoods. [函館山ロープウェイ](https://334.co.jp/) for service status and [五稜郭タワー](https://www.goryokaku-tower.co.jp/) for tower admission. |

| 小樽市 / Otaru | [小樽観光協会 walking guides](https://otaru.gr.jp/project/route) cover neighborhood, canal and port walks. |
| 洞爺湖町 / Toyako and the lakeshore | [洞爺湖温泉観光協会](https://www.laketoya.com/) for hot springs and lakeshore attractions. [有珠山ロープウェイ](https://usuzan.hokkaido.jp/ja/) for service status and operating hours in neighboring Sobetsu. |
