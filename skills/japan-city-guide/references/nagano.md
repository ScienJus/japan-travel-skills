# Nagano Prefecture and Nagano City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checks: 2026-11-02 (JST). This is a source check, not a publisher update date or
site-wide validation. Resolve 長野市 / Nagano City versus 長野県 / Nagano Prefecture from context.
For a station stay and local sightseeing, start with the city; for a broader prefectural trip,
add Go NAGANO and the actual destination's local body/operator.

## Prefectural discovery

Primary portal: [Go NAGANO](https://www.go-nagano.net/), the official prefectural tourism site.
The footer identifies 一般社団法人 長野県観光機構; the [prefectural government's site description](https://www.pref.nagano.lg.jp/kankoshin/go_nagano_proposal_1.html)
also establishes its official role.

| Need | Entry and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Regions | [エリアガイド](https://db.go-nagano.net/area-guide/?hsLang=ja-jp): resolve the intended region/municipality; the prefecture is not one city |

| Themes and practical context | Follow トリップアイデア and トラベルインフォ from the main portal; distinguish editorial ideas from operating facts |

The main portal links these database pages, and their entry bodies were readable in the source
check. `db.go-nagano.net` is an explicitly linked navigation destination, not an invented API or
an alternate client used after denial. Search controls/results may need more than a text fetch;
readable entry pages alone do not establish exhaustive filtered results.

## Nagano City

Primary portal: [ながの観光net](https://www.nagano-cvb.or.jp/), published by 公益財団法人
ながの観光コンベンションビューロー. Its homepage explicitly describes 長野市 and links its city
areas. This is the municipal travel portal, separate from Go NAGANO's prefectural scope.

| Need | Entry and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions by interest | [旅のテーマから探す](https://www.nagano-cvb.or.jp/modules/content/theme/): follow the relevant city theme to native detail records |
| Events | [イベントカレンダー](https://www.nagano-cvb.or.jp/modules/event/): verify date, venue, year and organizer |
| Suggested routes | [モデルコース](https://www.nagano-cvb.or.jp/modules/model/): keep mode and time assumptions; verify dated connections separately |
| Districts | Follow エリア from the homepage; city-center, 戸隠・飯綱 and other listed city areas are distinct destinations within a municipal guide |
| Access and maps | Follow アクセス and パンフレット from the homepage; transport schedules and PDF dates require their own checks |

The homepage and the linked attraction/event/model-course entry bodies were readable in the
source check. City-level scope is not synonymous with the station or downtown: the portal
includes 戸隠 and other outlying areas. Do not classify an excursion as outside Nagano City merely
because it is distant from the station, or assume it is a short walk because the city matches.

For a shortlisted temple, museum or mountain-area attraction, follow the listed operator for
admission/last-entry conditions, special events, seasonal access and closures. A general temple
visit and a particular paid hall, ceremony or guided experience can have different hours and
requirements. Apply the user's date without substituting a previous year's event or promising
access from a generic seasonal article.
