# Tokyo Metropolis and Tokyo urban sightseeing

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


Tokyo is a metropolis, not a present-day municipality called Tokyo City. For an urban Tokyo
request, start with the 23-ward areas; distinguish Tama and the islands before widening the trip.
Narita Airport and Tokyo Disney Resort are outside Tokyo Metropolis.

## Metropolitan discovery

[GO TOKYO](https://www.gotokyo.org/jp/) is the official Tokyo visitor portal. Its
[site information](https://www.gotokyo.org/jp/about-tcvb/index.html) and footer identify
公益財団法人東京観光財団 / Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau. The portal covers central Tokyo,
Tama, Izu and Ogasawara, so it serves both urban and wider metropolitan discovery.

## Tokyo urban areas

| Need | Entry and use |

| Areas and attractions | [SPOT](https://www.gotokyo.org/jp/spot/index.html): select the actual district; distinguish the urban areas from Tama/islands |

| Route ideas | [おすすめモデルコース](https://www.gotokyo.org/jp/story/walks-and-tours/index.html): check geography and mode before adopting a sequence |
| Access and visitor support | [旅の計画・交通情報](https://www.gotokyo.org/jp/plan/index.html): transport orientation and links to practical help |

Use district pages to narrow a large city before selecting facilities. Parks, museums and private
attractions have separate operators; follow their official links for notices and admission.
A model course's duration is an estimate, not a dated transfer or admission guarantee. Language
versions are linked from the portal; preserve the version actually read.
