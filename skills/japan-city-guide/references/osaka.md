# Osaka Prefecture and Osaka City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


Distinguish 大阪市 / Osaka City from 大阪府 / Osaka Prefecture. Sakai, Minoh and the
Kansai Airport area are not Osaka City sightseeing districts.

## Prefectural discovery

[OSAKA-INFO](https://osaka-info.jp/) is the official visitor portal of 大阪観光局 / Osaka
Convention & Tourism Bureau. Its footer and the linked
[bureau site](https://octb.osaka-info.jp/) identify the publisher. It covers the prefecture,
including Osaka City and Sakai; city and prefectural discovery share this portal.

## Osaka City

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions and experiences | [観光スポット・体験](https://osaka-info.jp/spot/): retain the native area and address, not only the Osaka label |
| Events | [イベント](https://osaka-info.jp/event/): select the correct occurrence and organizer's notices |
| Local specialties | [グルメ](https://osaka-info.jp/gourmet/): dish/culture introductions and venue leads, not independent quality scores |
| Routes | [モデルコース](https://osaka-info.jp/modelcourse/): distinguish central-city walks from Sakai and other wider itineraries |
| Transport orientation | [便利な交通手段](https://osaka-info.jp/information/useful-access/): general access; operators provide dated services and ticket conditions |
| Maps and support | Follow パンフレットダウンロード and 観光案内所 from the portal |

For city requests, narrow to the relevant city district before selecting records. Do not infer
current opening or a continuing exhibition from a past seasonal feature. Follow each facility's
manager link for closures and admission; use the actual ticket operator for pass eligibility.
The site labels some content as automatically translated, so check the source language when a
translated condition is material.
