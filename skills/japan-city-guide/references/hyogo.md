# Hyogo Prefecture and Kobe City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


神戸市 / Kobe City includes central districts and outlying areas such as Arima and Rokko.
Himeji and Awaji destinations are wider Hyogo trips, not Kobe city neighborhoods.

## Prefectural discovery

[兵庫観光ナビ](https://www.hyogo-tourism.jp/) is the official prefectural visitor portal.
Its [organization information](https://www.hyogo-tourism.jp/outline/) identifies
公益社団法人ひょうご観光本部. Use its area discovery when intentionally widening beyond Kobe.

## Kobe City

Primary portal: [Feel KOBE](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/). Its footer identifies 一般財団法人神戸観光局.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Areas | [エリアガイド](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/area-guide/): distinguish waterfront, central, mountain and hot-spring districts |
| Attractions | [スポット一覧](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/facilities/): select records and follow the responsible facility |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/event/): use the intended dates and organizer's current notices |
| Food and neighborhood themes | [コラム](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/column/): food/culture discovery; article dates and editorial status matter |
| Routes | [モデルコース](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/model_course/): check actual mode, extent and visit assumptions |
| Access | [アクセス](https://www.feel-kobe.jp/access/): orientation, with operator verification for a dated connection |

Central Kobe, Arima and mountain areas need different access and transfer allowances. Ropeways,
mountain transport, museums and gardens have their own operating calendars; a tourism course
does not verify them. Portal food articles can suggest venues but do not establish certification,
course price or meal availability. Keep the portal's transaction links outside this skill's work.
