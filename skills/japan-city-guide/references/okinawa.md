# Okinawa Prefecture and Naha City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


那覇市 / Naha City includes Shuri and its central districts; it is not all of Okinawa Island
or Okinawa Prefecture. Resorts in northern Okinawa Island and destinations on other islands
need wider discovery and their own transport arrangements.

## Prefectural discovery

[おきなわ物語](https://www.okinawastory.jp/) is the official prefectural visitor portal.
Its [site information](https://www.okinawastory.jp/page/site) identifies 一般財団法人
沖縄観光コンベンションビューロー (OCVB). Use its area and island categories for wider scope.
A listing on this prefectural portal does not mean the destination is in Naha or on the same island.

## Naha City

Primary portal: [NAHANAVI](https://www.naha-navi.or.jp/). Its
[association page](https://www.naha-navi.or.jp/sitepage/aboutus/) identifies 一般社団法人那覇市観光協会.
The root URL selected English in one read; explicit Japanese attraction/event entries and the
association body were readable. Follow actual language links and preserve the version used.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions and local areas | [那覇市の観光](https://www.naha-navi.or.jp/sightseeing/): inspect district, address and outside-city labels |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.naha-navi.or.jp/event/): read each occurrence's year; language versions can differ |
| Food leads | [Gourmet filter](https://www.naha-navi.or.jp/en/?s=&word%5B%5D=gourmet): English discovery view; check individual records rather than inferring current menus or exhaustive coverage |
| Access and sequence ideas | [Access to main tourist attractions](https://www.naha-navi.or.jp/en/sitepage/access/): English practical guide; recheck operator timetables |
| Maps, walks and local culture | Follow 観光ガイドマップ and the relevant sightseeing/category entry from NAHANAVI |

The English homepage's events section was marked in preparation and some English features were
old; do not use it to establish the current Japanese event calendar. For Shuri, inspect the park
operator's current accessible-area and restoration notices before suggesting entry. For markets
and food venues, distinguish the site's editorial feature from each operator's opening/meal rules.
Weather and marine-service cancellations need their own dated warning/operator sources.
