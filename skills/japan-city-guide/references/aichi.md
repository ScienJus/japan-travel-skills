# Aichi Prefecture and Nagoya City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


Use 名古屋市 / Nagoya City for a city request. Inuyama, Toyota and Nagakute are different
municipalities; attractions marketed for a Nagoya-based trip can be outside the city.

## Prefectural discovery

[Aichi Now](https://aichinow.pref.aichi.jp/) is the prefectural official visitor portal, also
linked by [Aichi Prefecture's tourism page](https://www.pref.aichi.jp/site/tourism/).
Use it for wider destinations and local tourism bodies. Retain the current government-domain
URL from official navigation rather than assuming older Aichi Now links remain unchanged.

## Nagoya City

Primary portal: [名古屋コンシェルジュ](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/). Its official-city label and
linked [publisher site](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/ncvb/) identify 公益財団法人
名古屋観光コンベンションビューロー.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions | [観光スポット](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/spot/): use the city district or explicit nearby-area scope |
| Events | [イベント](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/event/): check the occurrence and organizer's notice |
| Local dishes | [なごやめし](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/gourmet/digest/): dish introductions and venue discovery, not live restaurant inventory |
| Routes | [モデルコース](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/course/): sequencing ideas with mode and geography checks |
| Access | [アクセス](https://www.nagoya-info.jp/access/): regional/city movement orientation |
| Visitor support | Follow メーグル, 観光案内所 and 観光パンフレット from the primary portal |

The portal explicitly includes a nearby-area category; verify municipality before treating a
candidate as a city attraction. Industrial museums and tour programs need their own facility
notices, public-access conditions and reservation requirements. A tourism-bus route description
is not proof of the user's dated departure or that a ticket covers every selected leg.
