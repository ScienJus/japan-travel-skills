# Nara Prefecture and Nara City

## Scope and source checks

Entry/navigation checked: 2026-11-05 (JST). This is a source/link check, not publication
freshness, current admission or site-wide validation. Read selected records and operator notices live.


奈良市 / Nara City is distinct from 奈良県 / Nara Prefecture. Yoshino, Asuka, Sakurai and
Ikaruga are wider trips; a prefectural record is not automatically a Nara City match.

## Prefectural discovery

[あをによし なら旅ネット](https://yamatoji.nara-kankou.or.jp/) identifies itself as the official
prefectural visitor portal. Its footer identifies 一般財団法人奈良県ビジターズビューロー.
Use its regional categories for deliberate cross-city discovery. The Japanese homepage was
readable. An English portal read timed out during this check; English page bodies were not
verified, and search snippets do not supply their missing current facts.

## Nara City

Primary portal: [奈良市観光協会](https://narashikanko.or.jp/). The footer and
[association page](https://narashikanko.or.jp/association) identify 公益社団法人奈良市観光協会.

| Need | Entry and use |
| --- | --- |
| Attractions and experiences | [スポット・体験](https://narashikanko.or.jp/spot/): inspect municipality and the responsible temple/facility |
| Events | [行事・イベント](https://narashikanko.or.jp/event/): selected dates, special openings and organizer notices |
| Food culture | [グルメ](https://narashikanko.or.jp/gourmet): introductions and venue leads; verify individual meal conditions separately |
| Routes | [モデルコース](https://narashikanko.or.jp/course/): walking/transport ideas, not dated service proof |
| Access | [アクセス](https://narashikanko.or.jp/access/): general arrival and local movement guidance |
| Seasonal and local context | Follow 奈良の四季, 特集 and パンフレット from the city portal |

Temple grounds, halls, museums and special events can have separate admission rules. Daytime
access does not establish a night opening. A park introduction does not imply one closing rule
for every facility within it. Check the full attraction-to-onward-boarding chain when combining
a city visit with a transfer elsewhere.
