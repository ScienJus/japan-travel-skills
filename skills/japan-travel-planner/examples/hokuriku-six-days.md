# Worked example: plan, review, revise and hand off

This teaching example uses an invented brief and real Japanese places. Selected source facts were
checked on **2026-11-07 JST** for **2026-11-28 to 2026-12-03**. It is a dated illustration, not a
current itinerary, timetable feed or proof of effectiveness across models. Requery applicable
conditions for a real request; do not reuse these service references for another date.

## 1. Brief and planning choices

Two adults arrive at Nagano Station at 15:00 on November 28 and must reach Tokyo Station by 18:00
on December 3. They use public transport, carry medium luggage and prefer gardens, temples, local
food and an unhurried pace. 善光寺 and 兼六園 are must-visits; Takaoka should be a day trip if it fits.
They want two Kanazawa hotel candidates within a 1 km straight-line radius of the station reference
point, with a large communal bath. Meals need local-food directions, not named restaurant research
or availability checks. Once chosen, keep overnight dates and the return deadline fixed.

Choose **Nagano 1 night → Kanazawa 2 nights → Toyama 2 nights → Tokyo**, with Takaoka from Toyama.
This provides a full Kanazawa day and avoids carrying bags around Takaoka between hotel moves.
The extra local return journey is the trade-off. Nagano/Toyama hotel selection remains open;
station-area bases are assumptions, not existing bookings or confirmed luggage services.

**Rain adjustment:** shorten the garden and old-town outdoor portions rather than inserting an
unchecked museum. Dec 1 already contains the checked exhibition date. If Dec 2 is wet enough to
make Takaoka undesirable, shorten/cancel that optional day trip and use rest/station-area time;
do not move the museum back onto its closure date. A new named indoor venue would require its own
date-specific check. Daily forecasts and current foliage were not queried for this brief.

The [瑞龍寺 operator](https://www.zuiryuji.jp/index.html) publishes ordinary last entry 30 minutes
before closing and a later-December winter change; a morning visit avoids that cutoff. The
[prefectural Takaoka guide](https://www.info-toyama.com/stories/15city-takaoka) describes 富山–高岡
on あいの風とやま鉄道 as about 20 minutes. This is route orientation, not a dated outbound/return
query, and 高岡 is distinct from the Shinkansen station 新高岡. Specific trains and station-to-temple
movement remain to be checked when choosing that excursion's exact timing.

matched the 1 km circle on that page, and the first two source-ordered matches were retained.
This is not an exhaustive hotel recommendation or a ranking by quality.

Both remain **candidates**, not bookings. Walking claims are attributed to Jalan, not computed
from the distances. No stay availability, room quote or full official-property verification was done.
Choose the east-exit/onsen option or west-exit/bath option according to preference, then verify the
chosen room and stay terms manually. No hotel is silently selected by the example.

For meals, the [Nagano city portal](https://www.nagano-cvb.or.jp/),

user wants named venues, conditions or a budget-specific shortlist.

## 5. Handoff and the next change

The v2 tables and original links are the text handoff. A presentation can use: trip overview,
L1–L3 connections, one city section per base, Takaoka as a day trip, hotel choice and open decisions.
A map can filter city/area and hotels/attractions/stations; keep restaurant pins absent until actual
venues are selected. Only the two hotel coordinates and the Jalan reference point were obtained
here. Other places need sourced coordinates before placing pins. This example does not claim a
rendered PPTX, hosted map or complete geocoded itinerary.

If the user next asks to swap the two Toyama afternoons for rain, keep the museum on Dec 1 and
explain the closure constraint; swap only flexible outdoor/rest time. Update the affected daily
rows and any rendered views, leaving L1–L3 and overnight bases unchanged. Do not requery unchanged
trains simply to produce a new revision, or clear the outstanding luggage/local-transport checks.

Ready for the next user decisions: select hotels, decide whether to take the optional Takaoka
excursion and whether named restaurants are needed. Before travel, recheck applicable venue
notices, local connections, baggage arrangements and train availability/operations. The handoff
has checked critical facts and explicit gaps; it does not certify the whole trip.
